# India and Pakistan release boundary

This record describes the application's internal release gate. It is not a legal determination, government certificate, or claim that either country has approved RoktoKorobi. The original H1 source remains preserved in `india-pakistan-policy-page-drafts.md`. On 24 October the owner approved the current English copies, including their 24 October effective date; the four versioned files are now `india-privacy.md`, `india-terms.md`, `pakistan-privacy.md` and `pakistan-terms.md`. Production publication and live country activation remain distinct from preparing those files.

## Required before publishing an effective policy or accepting it

- Identify the responsible operator and a monitored contact and escalation route. The owner identified RoktoKorobi TM and the team mailbox. A physical service address was not supplied; the checker warns about it rather than assuming a universal address-disclosure requirement. Confirm any applicable local address requirement separately.
- Obtain final India and Pakistan privacy/terms text separately, with document version, exact content hash, effective date, language review appropriate to the audience, and written authorization by the responsible owner. The `contentAuthorizationReference` in the manifest is an internal authorization reference, **not** a government policy-approval certificate.
- Resolve every draft marker in the final copy. Do not substitute the prepared date for the effective date, or accept an H1 review revision as an approved version.
- Determine applicable local requirements and exact complaint, transfer, patient-data and medical-safety language with qualified India/Pakistan legal and medical reviewers. The checker warns when independent medical-review evidence is absent rather than treating an unspecified review document as a universal technical prerequisite. A hospital partnership is not assumed mandatory for a connection-only platform; whether any facility relationship or other clinical arrangement is required is a local-review question.

## Required before activating live personal-data and request features

- Demonstrate country isolation in deployed Firebase rules, privileged APIs/jobs, exports, direct URLs and authenticated sessions; a frontend filter alone is insufficient. The tested database rules were deployed 24 October while live country switches remained unset. Retain emulator and real-account negative-access test reports.
- Pending or rejected blood requests must not be readable by every verified user in a country. The local rule and request-list code use approved/open and owner-scoped queries. Cross-account emulator tests passed on 24 October; see the [technical verification report](./india-pakistan-technical-verification-2026-10-24.md). Deployed rules and the owner's manual acceptance remain separate from this local result.
- Demonstrate separately chosen map and partner-roster permissions; minimal projections; effective withdrawal; and country/organization-scoped leader access. Consent/acceptance history still needs a server-authenticated, versioned event design and an approved retention schedule.
- Approve the exact blood-request page and alert audiences, field previews, notification template, requester authority/closure workflow, and local medical escalation wording. Test country-specific delivery and withdrawal/notification preferences before deploying notification jobs. The new-country job has an additional `settings/countryNotifications/IN|PK` switch, which must remain unset/false until alert-specific approval and testing; enrollment launch alone cannot activate alerts.
- Assign accountable case handlers and a tested process for privacy requests, complaints, incidents and deletion/backup exceptions. Record actual provider products, hosting/backup regions and foreign staff access rather than inferring residency from a database path.
- Obtain explicit owner release authorization. For the 25 October owner-directed rollout, a completed real-user end-to-end report is a post-activation acceptance requirement, not a pre-activation gate; record the pending state honestly in the manifest and disable the affected country if live verification finds a safety or isolation failure. The [owner rollout direction](./country-owner-rollout-direction-2026-10-25.md) records this narrow exception. Cloud Functions for automatic country stats cannot currently be deployed because the Firebase project requires a Blaze plan; no billing-plan change was made.

## Recommendations, not universal legal prerequisites

- A government “policy approval certificate” is **not** required by this checker. Add such a requirement only if local counsel identifies an applicable instrument and exact obligation.
- A formal hospital partnership is **not** presumed necessary merely because the platform connects people. Medical review of the actual service model and claims remains necessary.
- A self-service deletion button can follow a verified manual workflow. Extra translations beyond informed access and applicable requirements, cosmetic enhancements, and optional map/roster rollout may be deferred. If an optional feature is disabled, do not claim it works or require its feature-specific test as a prerequisite to publishing an otherwise accurate policy.

The dedicated check is `node scripts/check-country-release.mjs IN` or `PK`. Its incomplete manifest must fail the production release decision, but it is intentionally not part of `next build` or local unit tests. A passing manifest check alone is not legal approval, medical clearance, or proof of deployed behavior.

For the distinction between contact information and a physical address, see India's [Digital Personal Data Protection Act, section 8(9)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/02/Digital-Personal-Data-Protection-Act-2023.pdf), which calls for business contact information of a person able to answer data-processing questions; this does not by itself settle every applicable disclosure duty. Pakistan's ministry [lists the May 2023 Personal Data Protection Bill as a draft](https://www.moitt.gov.pk/Legislations). These sources do not establish a blanket nonprofit exemption or supply project-specific legal clearance.
