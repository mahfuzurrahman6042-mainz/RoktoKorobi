# Pakistan Privacy Policy
Version: PK-PRIV-1.0
Prepared: 21 October 2026
Effective date: 24 October 2026

## 1. Who provides the service
RoktoKorobi TM is a student-led initiative providing a platform for potential donors, people seeking blood, and partner communities. This policy concerns the Pakistan service.


The local RoktoKorobi team coordinates this country service. Individual team-member names are not published.

Contact for privacy requests and complaints: [joinroktokorobinetwork@gmail.com](mailto:joinroktokorobinetwork@gmail.com). The RoktoKorobi team will review messages received there. Individual delegate names are not published as the complaint route.

## 2. What the platform does
RoktoKorobi provides connection and community-management tools. Delegates onboard users, and partner communities manage their own activity. Donors decide whether to respond. RoktoKorobi does not undertake case-by-case donor matching or guarantee a donation.

Registration, a map listing or a blood-request response does not certify medical eligibility or compatibility. Actual donation and screening take place through an appropriate medical facility.

## 3. Information and purposes

| Feature | Information the app collects or generates | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account and sign-in | Name, email, phone, country, account identifier and verification state | Create an account and apply country-scoped access |
| Potential-donor profile | Blood group, date of birth, weight, last donation date, optional medical-condition notes and entered location | Support voluntary donor discovery; not medical screening |
| Blood request | Patient name, requester contact, hospital, location, blood group, urgency, message and status | Display and manage a blood request |
| Donor map | Separate map choice, generic donor label, blood group and approximate city or area | Optional approximate-location discovery within the selected country |
| Organization roster | Separate roster choice, organization reference, member identifier, name and join date | Show consenting members to their organization's authorized leader |
| Communications and operations | Support messages, notification preference or token, and technical logs when generated | Respond to enquiries, operate approved alerts and protect the service |
| Choices and requests | Policy-acceptance field, map and roster preferences, withdrawal and support-request records when generated | Record and implement user choices |

Do not provide passwords, one-time codes, identity-document images or unnecessary patient records in support messages. Avoid adding detailed medical histories to free-text fields.

## 4. What other people can see
Approved blood requests can be visible to verified users in Pakistan. Notifications, when enabled, are directed to same-country potential donors with a matching blood group and notification tokens, subject to their recorded preferences. Notifications may fail or arrive late and never guarantee a response. Pending and rejected requests are visible only to the requester and authorized administrators.

The donor map is designed to show a generic label, blood group and approximate area, not a donor's name or phone number. Even an approximate area may reveal information when combined with other details.

An organization leader's roster is limited to members who opted in to that organization's list. It includes name and join date, not phone number. A referral code does not authorize access to another organization's roster.

Privacy complaints go to the team mailbox above. They are not sent to the blood-request notification audience.

## 5. Choices and consent
Map participation and partner-roster participation use distinct, optional choices. Refusing either choice does not prevent ordinary account use. The related screens explain which information is shown and to whom.

You can withdraw each optional choice through the available account controls or by emailing the team. Withdrawal stops future optional display, but cannot recall screenshots, previously delivered alerts or copies already made by another recipient.


## 6. Information about another person and children
A requester should have appropriate authority to submit another person's information. Provide only what is needed for the request, and avoid identifying a patient when the request can work without it.

Ordinary accounts and partner accounts do not require a donor-age declaration. Donor registration requires an age of at least 18 and is not medical eligibility assessment. School partnerships do not automatically authorize collecting information about another person; users should submit only information they are authorized to provide.

## 7. Providers, hosting and international access
The app uses Firebase/Google services and Vercel hosting. India, Pakistan and Bangladesh records use country-scoped paths in a shared database, not three physically separate databases.

Country-scoped access does not mean that data is physically stored in that country. Hosting, support access and backups may involve processing outside the user's country. RoktoKorobi TM says it does not sell personal data.

## 8. Retention and deletion
No fixed retention period or backup-deletion period is currently stated for this country service. Contact the team to request deletion and to ask how a particular record is retained.

Removing a map pin, removing a roster entry and deleting an account are different requests. Contact the team mailbox above to specify what you want removed.

## 9. Requests about your information
You may contact [joinroktokorobinetwork@gmail.com](mailto:joinroktokorobinetwork@gmail.com) to request access, correction, an export, account deletion, removal from the donor map or withdrawal from an organization roster. State your country and request type. Do not send unnecessary sensitive documents.

Account deletion may be requested by email; a self-service deletion control is not currently offered here.


## 10. Complaints and timeframes
For privacy complaints, contact the team mailbox above. No fixed response or resolution time is promised.

Messages may be submitted at any time. The mailbox is not an emergency medical service.

## 11. Security and incidents
RoktoKorobi does not claim a security certification, end-to-end encryption or guaranteed protection. If you suspect a security issue, contact the team mailbox above.

## 12. Changes and contact
Material policy changes will be described in a new dated version. Continuing to browse does not mean consenting to new optional processing.
